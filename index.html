01 • Career Foundation

Career Guide: Product Manager (PM) vs. Project Manager (PjM)

Job titles across companies are inconsistent. We classify roles by their primary management object and ultimate accountability—not title keywords alone.

PM • Product Manager Object: The Product
“What product should we build, and why?”

Ultimate Responsibility: Product value and business/user outcomes; ensuring the product solves the right problems.

Core Responsibilities:
    PjM • Project Manager Object: The Project
    “How do we deliver the agreed work within scope, time, and resource constraints?”

    Ultimate Responsibility: Project objectives and on-time, within-budget, quality delivery across stakeholders.
